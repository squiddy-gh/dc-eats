# NoVA Top 50 Restaurants 2026 — collection notes

Source: [Northern Virginia Magazine’s 2026 list](https://northernvirginiamag.com/food-and-drink/reviews/2026/10/21/the-50-best-restaurants-in-northern-virginia-for-2026/), published October 21, 2026.

The dataset preserves the magazine’s numbered Top 10 and labels the remaining selections as Top 50. Cuisine, price, and “Eat This” recommendations are transcribed from the article. Descriptions are short factual summaries written for this map rather than copied reviews.

Addresses, coordinates, websites, phones, and previously verified opening hours were carried forward from the 2025 dataset when the restaurant and location matched. The 2026 article names both locations for Carbonara, Clarity, Ruthie’s All-Day, and Sense of Thai; all are mapped, producing 54 locations for 50 selections. New venues were checked against their official websites where available. Coordinates are address matches and should be reviewed if a pin appears misplaced.

The 2025 CSV and guide entry remain unchanged. The 2026 layer uses a blue `N` marker that evokes NoVA; the older edition keeps its olive `50` marker. The full guide labels supply the years without crowding the pins.
